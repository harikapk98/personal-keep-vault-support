Privacy policy

Personal Keep Vault · Version 1.0 Friends & Family beta

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Your documents stay on your device

The app stores imported originals, text read from documents, review drafts, confirmed records, relationships and reminders in its local storage on your iPhone or Mac. Reading and search run on the device. The app does not send your documents or questions to the developer, an AI provider, an advertising service or an analytics service. Private iCloud sync is disabled in this beta.

What the app uses

The app accesses only documents or photographs you select and camera scans you choose to make. It copies imported files; it does not rename or modify your source files. Your selected appearance and device-unlock preference are stored locally. No online account is created by opening the vault.

Device unlock and protection

Optional device unlock uses Apple’s authentication system. The app does not receive your Face ID data or device passcode. The app requests iOS file protection for its saved records and originals and hides content when inactive. Device security also depends on your iPhone’s passcode and operating system.

Retention and deletion

Records and drafts remain on this iPhone until you delete them or remove the app’s data. Delete a document from its detail screen; the confirmation explains which associated information and reminders will be removed. Your original file outside the app is not deleted. Closing the vault does not erase stored documents. Deleting the app can erase its local records.

Backup and recovery

This Friends & Family beta does not provide app-level iCloud sync or family record sharing. Keep independent copies of source documents. Local recovery exports are not a verified replacement-device restore workflow. Deleting the app can erase its records. Apple device backups may separately include app data depending on your settings.

Sharing, tracking and purchases

Family sharing is not enabled in this release. There are no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics integrations and no in-app purchases. The developer does not receive your document contents through the app. Apple may separately process App Store, device-backup or diagnostic information according to your Apple settings and Apple’s policies.

Sensitive information

This is not a password manager. Do not import SSNs, passwords, PINs, recovery codes, private keys or full banking or payment-card credentials. Detection is not exhaustive. Original documents retain their original contents, even when a summary masks an identifier. Review all extracted suggestions before confirming them.

Policy changes

Changes to data handling will be described in an updated policy. Cloud processing, online accounts or sharing would require updated disclosures before those features are enabled.
